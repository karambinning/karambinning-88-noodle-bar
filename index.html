eight.

In Cantonese the number eight sounds like fā — to prosper. Double it, and you have fā fā: prosperity, twice over. It's a number written above market stalls in Hong Kong and slipped onto licence plates in Kowloon. It's a wish, set in numerals.

We took the number for the noodle bar on Cross Church Street, opposite the parish church, in the middle of Huddersfield. A short Cantonese menu — noodles, rice, sweet and sour, side orders — cooked to order, sent out for takeaway, or eaten in at the counter.

Two digits. One kitchen. Open through the evening.
